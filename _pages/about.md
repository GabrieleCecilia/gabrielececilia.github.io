I am a PhD student at the School of Computer and Cyber Sciences at Augusta University. My advisor is [Dr. Clément Aubert](https://spots.augusta.edu/caubert/).

My research interests lie within the broader fields of formal methods and theoretical computer science, with a focus on process algebra, interactive theorem proving, reversible computation and concurrency. 

I am currently working on the [Concurrency in Reversible Computations](https://github.com/CinRC) project, whose aim is to improve the current specification languages for concurrent reversible programs. In particular, I am working on the formalization of reversible concurrent calculi with proof assistants such as Rocq and Beluga.
